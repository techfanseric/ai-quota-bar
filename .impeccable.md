## Design Context

### Users

AI Quota Bar is used by people running long Codex or OpenAI workloads on a nearby Mac. They need to confirm task activity, quota headroom, route health, active connections, and sleep-protection status without returning to the computer.

### Browser status display

- Primary reference viewport: iPhone 13 mini landscape, 812 × 375 CSS pixels, including notches and safe-area insets.
- Additional devices: tablets, vehicle infotainment browsers, and other computers on the same reachable LAN. The dashboard is browser-based, not phone-exclusive. PWA support depends on browser capability.
- Secondary viewport: portrait phones. Portrait may scroll when necessary, but all status information must remain available.
- Usage context: a phone can remain several feet away as an always-on, glanceable monitoring display. Controls are secondary; information and state dominate.
- Visual voice: dense, financial, restrained — closer to a compact stock-market quote board than a card dashboard.
- Theme: true black OLED canvas with sparing, muted green, amber, blue, and red signals. Avoid large fixed bright regions, decorative glow, gradients, glass, shadows, and repeated rounded cards.
- Hierarchy: WORKING / IDLE and remaining quota are the visual center. Route and active-link telemetry support them. Protection runs as a single-line ticker at the bottom.
- Legibility: core state and quota values must be readable from a distance. Labels may be compact, but should not force the important values below comfortable mobile sizes.
- Motion: a distinctive remaining-capacity column plus energy-wave loop communicates WORKING. IDLE remains unmistakable with a slower scanning/resting rhythm. Animate only transform and opacity, keep motion inexpensive, and provide a static `prefers-reduced-motion` presentation.
- OLED care: shift the full composition by only a few pixels, periodically rotate the order of repeated quota content, continuously move the low-intensity protection ticker, and briefly lift the opacity of changed state without leaving a large high-brightness surface fixed in place.
- Interaction: read-only by default. Any necessary action, such as enabling an experimental keep-awake fallback, must respect a 44 × 44 CSS pixel touch target.

### Desktop menu bar

- Users are developers who keep AI Quota Bar in the macOS menu bar and need to understand coding-plan quota, pace, and connectivity in a very quick glance without opening the detailed menu.
- The macOS menu bar is a separate compact context, not a layout template for the phone. It favors instant pointer access, native typography, short labels, and the minimum footprint expected of a menu extra.
- Use compact monochrome geometry that follows the system label color and opacity hierarchy in both light and dark appearances. Preserve the 22-point glance target, avoid ornamental motion, and make mirrored deficit/reserve states optically consistent.
- Preserve stable visual semantics: the outer ring represents Weekly remaining, while the split center represents pace direction and magnitude.
- Use the system's consumed-track tint for inactive or already-consumed geometry. Connectivity loss overrides live quota rendering without introducing competing motion.
- Keep continuous animation lightweight and limited to the element that needs attention, with a clear Reduce Motion equivalent.
- Mobile may borrow the menu bar's remaining-quota column metaphor, but must scale its state and data for distance viewing and touch-safe display.

### Brand Personality

Calm, vigilant, and instrument-like. The product should feel like dependable monitoring equipment rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Design Principles

1. Glance first: state and quota are identifiable before supporting text is read.
2. One landscape frame: fit the meaningful dashboard into the iPhone 13 mini landscape safe area.
3. Information over chrome: use rules, alignment, and rhythm instead of card stacks or numbered sections.
4. OLED-aware, not dim: maintain readable normal brightness while moving and varying the smallest useful amount.
5. Honest status: differentiate working, idle, degraded protection, and disconnected states without relying on color alone.

### Product homepage hero

- Show reset announcements, quota/usage and task/connection panels together. Main capabilities must not depend on visitors discovering tabs or switching preview modes.
- Re-create the current left-click menu structure from source: Codex resets first, then the provider/model summary and account quota/usage. Keep header acknowledgement/history/refresh and row source/read actions as compact native icons.
- Demonstrate notification arrival alongside those menus. Viewing does not acknowledge; marking read keeps the menu open; source links close it. Explicitly label sample data and the fact that left/right menus open separately in the app.

- Notification previews float over the menus without reserving a separate layout slot. Clicking the banner or its view action retracts it and focuses the menu inbox while preserving unread; explicit acknowledgement alone stops reminders.
