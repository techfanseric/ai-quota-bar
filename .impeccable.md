## Design Context

### Users

Developers who keep AI Quota Bar in the macOS menu bar and need to understand coding-plan quota, pace, and connectivity in a very quick glance without opening the detailed menu.

### Brand Personality

Native, restrained, and precise. The interface should feel at home in macOS, communicate state before decoration, and remain calm during normal operation while making exceptional states unmistakable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use compact monochrome geometry that follows the system label color and opacity hierarchy in both light and dark appearances. Preserve the 22-point glance target, avoid ornamental motion, and make mirrored deficit/reserve states optically consistent.

### Design Principles

- Preserve stable visual semantics: the outer ring represents Weekly remaining, while the split center represents pace direction and magnitude.
- Use the system's consumed-track tint for inactive or already-consumed geometry.
- Prefer one strong cue per state; connectivity loss overrides live quota rendering without introducing competing motion.
- Keep continuous animation lightweight and limited to the element that needs attention.
- Respect Reduce Motion with a clear static equivalent.
