# v1.38.4 — Make low-quota warnings easier to see

macOS bug-fix release, build 80.

- Replaces the muted yellow low-quota accent with saturated orange: a deeper orange in light appearances and a brighter orange in dark appearances.
- Applies the warning color to the provider initial and active center geometry as well as the remaining arc, keeping the warning visible even when quota approaches zero.
- Provider initials also turn red for failures and offline states. Normal states retain native template tint, and the quota and pace geometry retain their existing meaning.
- 77 related tests passed, covering light/dark appearances, warning visibility at 0%/1%/5%/20%, failure precedence, recovery to template tint and both task animation layouts.
