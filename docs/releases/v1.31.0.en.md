# v1.31.0 — Team view in the menu usage card

macOS build 60.

## The menu usage card can now show the whole team

- The Codex usage card in the left-click menu gains a Local / Team switch (shown once a team connection exists): Local keeps reading the on-device scan, while Team reads server-aggregated current-month usage and the rolling last-24-hours timeline.
- The team view ships a member picker: "Whole team" by default, or drill down to a single member to see their month heatmap and 24-hour distribution.
- The team view does not require a local Codex sign-in — the card stays usable without one and only reads what members report via "Share local usage"; an empty-state hint appears until reports arrive.
- Server aggregates move far slower than the menu's once-a-minute re-render, so each fetch is reused for five minutes; reopening the menu or switching member/team refreshes immediately. When a member leaves the team the selection falls back to "Whole team", and disconnecting the team returns to the local view.
- While the first fetch is in flight the "Last 24h" caption already shows, matching the server's fixed 24-hour hourly window so the layout does not jump.

Requires macOS 14+; the DMG targets Apple Silicon and keeps the ad-hoc signature without Apple notarization.
