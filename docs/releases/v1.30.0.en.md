# v1.30.0 — Login-free Codex account switching

macOS build 59.

## Switch Codex accounts without logging in again

- The right-click panel gains a "Codex accounts" section: the active account and every stashed backup are listed, and clicking Switch performs an atomic rename swap of `~/.codex/auth.json` against the account pool (`~/.codex/accounts/`) — no login prompts, no QR codes.
- "Log in a new account" stashes the current credentials into the pool, then reopens ChatGPT for you to sign in once; from then on the new account is one click away.
- Guard rails: the app never quits ChatGPT/Codex on your behalf. While the desktop app or a `codex` CLI process is running, a switch request parks itself with a banner ("quit ChatGPT — the switch finishes automatically") and completes by itself within seconds of you quitting normally. The running state is re-checked at the last moment before any file moves.
- The pool is manageable in place: rename a backup, delete one (moves to Trash), or import earlier hand-made `auth *.json` backups with one click. A same-account re-login replaces the older copy instead of duplicating it, and API-key-mode auth files are never touched automatically.
- Each account row shows the last quota captured while that account was active: 5-hour and Weekly remaining percentages with their reset times (hover for full timestamps). Quota snapshots are recorded per account digest on every successful refresh.
- Under the hood, auth-file parsing now lives in the shared local-usage core (same rules as the usage-account observation); the switch itself is two atomic renames that preserve 0600 permissions, and no token content is ever read or stored — only the account digest and email.

Requires macOS 14+; the DMG targets Apple Silicon and keeps the ad-hoc signature without Apple notarization.
